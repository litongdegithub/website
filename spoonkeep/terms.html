1. What Spoonkeep does

Spoonkeep saves recipes you find (in social media posts, on web pages, in screenshots and photos, or typed by you) into a library on your phone, and helps you plan meals, make a shopping list and cook. It copies what the source says; it doesn’t invent recipes.

2. Recipes read by AI

  • Screenshots, photos, pasted text and shared videos are read by AI. Spoonkeep checks every amount against the source and leaves an amount empty when the source doesn’t give one, but reading can still be wrong or incomplete. Check a recipe before you cook it, especially amounts, cooking times and temperatures.
  • Spoonkeep doesn’t know what is in the products you buy. If you or anyone you cook for has an allergy or intolerance, read product labels and take the usual care.
  • Translations and unit conversions are automatic and may not be exact.

3. Other people’s recipes

  • Recipes you save belong to the people who made them. Spoonkeep keeps a copy on your phone for your personal use, with a link back to the source where there is one.
  • Don’t use Spoonkeep to copy recipes in bulk or to republish other people’s content. Only import content you have the right to use for yourself.
  • Spoonkeep isn’t affiliated with TikTok, Instagram, Paprika or any website you save from. Their names are the trademarks of their owners.

4. Free use and Spoonkeep Pro

  • Saving recipes from web pages, typing them in, importing from Paprika, planning, shopping and cooking are free, with no limit on how many recipes you keep. Without a subscription you also get 10 AI imports in total (shared posts, screenshots, photos or pasted text).
  • Spoonkeep Pro removes the limit on AI imports, subject to fair use (section 5). It is an auto-renewing subscription: yearly ($29.99 in the US, €24.99 in euro countries) or monthly ($5.99 in the US, €4.99 in euro countries). Prices in other countries are shown in the app before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before it.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: use reportaproblem.apple.com. This doesn’t affect any right of withdrawal or refund you have under the law where you live.

5. Fair use

“Unlimited” AI imports are for one person saving recipes for their own cooking. To keep the service working for everyone, Pro allows up to 60 AI imports per device per day. Don’t use scripts or other automated means to access the app or our server, or try to get around the free allowance or the subscription. We may limit, suspend or end access for abuse.

6. Your recipes on your phone

Your recipes, cookbooks, plans and lists are stored only on your phone. If you delete the app or lose your phone without a device backup, we can’t recover them.

7. Changes to the service

We may add, change or remove features, and sites or platforms may change in ways that stop some imports from working. If we plan to shut Spoonkeep down for good, we’ll tell you in the app in advance.

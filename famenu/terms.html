1. What Famenu does

Famenu plans lunch and dinner for your household from its own library of recipes, adapts each dish for the people who need a different version, makes a shopping list with estimated prices, and guides you through the cooking. It is a planning aid, not medical or dietary advice.

2. Allergies, diets and nutrition

  • Famenu leaves out the allergens and foods you tell it to, as far as the recipes as written allow. It can’t know what is in the products you buy: packaged foods can contain allergens or traces of them, and recipes can change. Always read product labels and take the usual care for anyone with an allergy or intolerance.
  • Nutrition per serving is worked out from USDA FoodData Central values for each dish as written. Real values depend on the products, quantities and cooking, so treat them as estimates. Famenu doesn’t make health claims. For medical diets, follow the advice of a doctor or dietitian.
  • Cook safely: follow cooking times and temperatures, and supervise children in the kitchen.

3. Prices

Prices in the shopping list are estimates per pack for your country and the kind of supermarket you choose. Real prices vary by shop, region and week, and you can change any price in the app. Supermarket names in the app only describe a price level; Famenu has no affiliation with those companies, and their names are the trademarks of their owners.

4. Free use and Famenu Plus

  • Without a subscription, the first three planned days of your first week are free, with every recipe, the shopping list and cooking mode.
  • Famenu Plus is an auto-renewing subscription: yearly ($34.99 in the US, £29.99 in the UK, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers) or monthly ($9.99 in the US, £7.99 in the UK). Prices in other countries are shown in the app before you buy. Plus plans every day of every week, lets you plan again whenever you like and plan next week ahead.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase, or when the free trial ends. The subscription renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before it. Famenu reminds you two days before a free trial ends, if you allow notifications.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). Cancelling stops the next renewal; Plus stays until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you won’t be charged.
  • Famenu Plus can be shared with your family through Apple’s Family Sharing.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: use reportaproblem.apple.com. This doesn’t affect any right of withdrawal or refund you have under the law where you live.

6. Your data on your phone

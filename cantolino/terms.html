1. What Cantolino does

Cantolino helps you sing in tune. It listens while you sing exercises and songs, shows the pitch of your voice against the notes, and plays guide notes and an accompaniment. It also has a voice test, your progress over time, before-and-after recordings, choir parts and the import of your own scores.

2. Scores are estimates

Pitch detection and scoring are done automatically on your phone. They are estimates: noise, the phone’s speaker, a weak microphone or headphones can make them miss or misjudge a note. Cantolino doesn’t certify musical skill and doesn’t replace a singing teacher.

3. Look after your voice

  • Warm up gently, sing at a comfortable volume, and don’t push your voice above or below what feels easy. The app puts songs in your range, and you can change your range at any time.
  • Rest your voice and drink water. Stop if singing hurts, or if you feel hoarse, strained or out of breath.
  • If hoarseness or pain lasts more than two weeks, or you have a voice or health condition, talk to a doctor or a voice specialist. Cantolino gives no medical advice.

  • The instrument sounds come from the Versilian Community Sample Library (CC0), with the organ recorded by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio, and from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios (guitar). The accordion is made by us.

CineDrive’s “Places you’ve driven to” fills in the countries, states and areas your drives passed through. The outlines come from these open datasets. They were simplified for use on a phone; the shapes shown are approximate and say nothing about any border dispute.

Countries, states and provinces

Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com (public domain).

City-level areas

Via geoBoundaries (gbOpen; China from gbHumanitarian): Runfola, D. et al. (2020) geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative boundaries. PLoS ONE 15(4): e0231866. Each country’s data comes from its own source under its own open licence:

  • Australia: Australian Bureau of Statistics, 2022. CC BY 4.0.
  • Canada: Statistics Canada, 2022. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada 2.0.
  • China (prefecture-level cities): publicly available online sources, HDX (OCHA), 2020. CC BY 3.0 IGO.
  • France: Institut national de l’information géographique et forestière (IGN-F), 2022. Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0 (Etalab).
  • Germany: © GeoBasis-DE / Bundesamt für Kartographie und Geodäsie (BKG), 2021. Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung – Version 2.0.
  • Italy: ISTAT, Istituto Nazionale di Statistica, 2023. CC BY 3.0.
  • Netherlands: Nationaal Georegister, 2022. CC0 1.0.
  • Spain: Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), 2018. Source: INE, under the INE data licence.
  • United Kingdom: Office for National Statistics, 2019. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right.
  • United States: U.S. Census Bureau, MAF/TIGER database, 2018. Public domain.

The outlines were simplified (fewer points, small islands left out). Country, province, ocean and sea names on the footprint maps, and where they stand, are from Natural Earth too, as are the rivers and lakes drawn on them. None of these sources endorse CineDrive.

Place photos

The photos on the States cards (where you haven’t chosen your own) come from Wikivoyage and are hosted on Wikimedia Commons. Each belongs to its author and is used under its own free licence (for example CC BY-SA or CC BY); press and hold a photo in the app to see its author and licence. Wikivoyage and Wikimedia do not endorse CineDrive.

The Earth from above

Imagery courtesy of NASA’s Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA’s Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS): Blue Marble shaded relief and bathymetry (NASA Earth Observatory) for the footprint maps, its colours adjusted by CineDrive, and VIIRS Black Marble (NASA Earth Observatory, Suomi NPP) for the Earth at night. NASA imagery is in the public domain; NASA does not endorse CineDrive.

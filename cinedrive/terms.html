1. What CineDrive does

CineDrive records your drives on your phone, makes a short film of each one, lights up the roads you have driven on a map, and shows how much of some famous roads you have driven. It is a personal log and keepsake. It is not a navigation, safety or speed device: it doesn’t guide you or warn you about anything on the road, and it never shows or ranks speed. CineDrive is meant for adults who drive and isn’t intended for children.

2. Drive safely

  • Never handle your phone while driving. CineDrive records on its own, so there is nothing to do during a drive. If you start or end a drive by hand, do it before you set off or after you park.
  • Follow traffic laws and road signs, and drive for the conditions. Nothing in the app is a reason to drive somewhere, or to drive in a particular way.
  • Information about legendary roads may be out of date, and their shapes in the app are approximate. Roads can close or change, and conditions vary with the season and the weather. Check current conditions, closures and local rules before you drive one.
  • You are always responsible for how and where you drive.

3. Recordings, maps and road data

  • Automatic recording depends on iOS, your permissions and settings (such as Low Power Mode) and the GPS signal. A drive can be missed, start late, end early or include a stretch that wasn’t driving. You can always start a drive by hand.
  • Distances, times and places are estimates from GPS. They aren’t meant as an official record, for example for mileage claims, tax or insurance.
  • Maps and place names are provided by Apple. Legendary road data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

4. Your photos, cars and films

  • You keep all rights to your photos and films. Only add photos you have the right to use.
  • A film shows the route and place names of a drive. Before you share one publicly, think about whether it shows where you live or work.
  • Films include map imagery from Apple. Share them for personal use, for example with friends or on your own social accounts, not for advertising or other commercial purposes.

5. Free use and CineDrive Pro

  • Free, for as long as you use CineDrive: unlimited drives, films in the Night style with a small watermark, legendary roads, and up to 2 cars in the garage.
  • CineDrive Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly. It adds the Atlas and Grid film styles, removes the watermark and lets you keep as many cars as you like. The price for your country and the billing period are shown in the app before you buy, and that is the price that applies.

  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions), or from Garage → Settings → Manage subscription in the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for. After that, your drives stay on your phone and new films are made in the Night style with the watermark.

  • Refunds are handled by Apple: use reportaproblem.apple.com. This doesn’t affect any right of withdrawal or refund you have under the law where you live.

7. Your data on your phone

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Shotwise (“Shotwise”, “we”, “us”) turns a video you choose into a written breakdown: style, shot list, actions, sound and a remake checklist. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, where it goes and how long we keep it.

The short version

  • No sign-up. We don’t ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • The video you pick is sent to AI services for analysis and deleted from our servers as soon as it has been analyzed.
  • Your report stays on our servers only until the app picks it up, 24 hours at most. After that it’s only on your phone.
  • We keep simple usage records (no content) for 90 days, and your purchased balance for as long as you have one.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

What we handle and why

  1. Anonymous identifiers. On first launch the app creates a random device ID, kept in your phone’s keychain, and an anonymous account on our backend. We use them to count your free and purchased breakdowns and to prevent abuse. They aren’t linked to your name or contact details. The app only shows a short “support code” made from the device ID.
  2. Videos you choose. When you start a breakdown, the app uploads that one video (picture and sound) to our server, and our AI providers analyze it. We delete it from our storage as soon as the analysis ends, whether it succeeds or not. An upload that is never analyzed, for example because you closed the app, is deleted within about an hour. Please don’t upload videos showing sensitive personal information.
  3. Breakdown reports. A report is stored on our server until the app fetches it, and deleted after 24 hours at the latest. Your history of reports is saved only on your phone.
  4. Usage and purchase records. For each breakdown we record when it ran, which AI model was used, whether it worked and how many tokens it used, with no content, to manage costs and limits. For purchases we keep your credit balance and the store transaction IDs, so credits are added exactly once. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  5. Support emails. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

We don’t collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any photos or videos other than the one you pick.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase runs our backend (database, file storage and server functions), hosted in Singapore.
  • OpenRouter (United States) passes your video to Alibaba Cloud’s Qwen model for analysis. We ask OpenRouter to use only providers that don’t store your content or train on it. Their own privacy policies also apply.
  • Apple handles in-app purchases.

These services operate in different countries, so your data may be processed outside the country you live in, including in Singapore, the United States and China. We share only what each service needs to do its job.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: in the app, go to Me → Delete my data. This deletes your usage records, any pending report and your anonymous account on our servers, and clears the history on your phone. We keep your remaining purchased credits and whether the free breakdown was used, linked only to the random device ID, so you don’t lose what you paid for.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us with your support code.
  • You can withdraw your consent to sending videos to AI at any time by deleting your data. The app asks for consent again before it sends anything.

Children

Shotwise isn’t intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Uploaded videos are kept in private storage that only our server can access, and access to our systems is restricted.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above. If a change affects what happens to your videos, the app will show you the new terms before you continue.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 21 日

Shotwise（以下称“Shotwise”或“我们”）可以把你选择的视频拆解成文字：风格、分镜、动作、声音和翻拍清单。本政策说明 App 会处理哪些数据、为什么处理、发送给谁以及保存多久。

简要说明

  • 无需注册，我们不会索取你的姓名、手机号或邮箱。
  • 你选择的视频会发送给 AI 服务进行分析，分析完成后立即从我们的服务器删除。
  • 拆解报告只在服务器上暂存到 App 取回为止，最多 24 小时，之后只保存在你的手机上。
  • 我们保留简单的使用记录（不含任何内容）90 天，已购次数在你使用完之前一直保留。
  • 我们不出售你的数据，不展示广告，也不跨 App 追踪你。

我们处理哪些数据，以及为什么

  1. 匿名标识。首次打开时，App 会生成一个随机的设备 ID（保存在手机钥匙串中），并在我们的后端创建一个匿名账号，用来计算你的免费次数和已购次数，并防止滥用。它们不与你的姓名或联系方式关联。App 里只显示由设备 ID 生成的简短“支持码”。
  2. 你选择的视频。开始拆解时，App 会把这一个视频（包括画面和声音）上传到我们的服务器，由我们的 AI 服务商进行分析。无论分析成功与否，分析结束后都会立即从存储中删除。上传后没有被分析的视频（例如中途关掉了 App）会在大约 1 小时内删除。请不要上传包含个人敏感信息的视频。
  3. 拆解报告。报告在服务器上保存到 App 取回为止，最迟 24 小时后删除。你的拆解记录只保存在手机上。
  4. 使用和购买记录。每次拆解，我们会记录时间、使用的 AI 模型、是否成功和消耗的 token 数（不含任何内容），用于控制成本和限额。购买方面，我们保留你的次数余额和商店交易编号，确保每笔购买只到账一次。付款由 Apple 处理，我们看不到你的支付信息。
  5. 客服邮件。如果你给我们发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容，仅用于回复你。

我们不收集你的位置、通讯录、广告标识符，也不会读取你选择的那个视频以外的任何照片或视频。

谁会处理你的数据

  • Supabase：我们的后端服务（数据库、文件存储和服务器函数），部署在新加坡。
  • OpenRouter（美国）：把你的视频转交给阿里云的通义千问模型进行分析。我们要求 OpenRouter 只使用不保存内容、不拿内容训练的服务商。它们也按各自的隐私政策处理数据。
  • Apple：处理 App 内购买。

这些服务分布在不同国家和地区，因此你的数据可能在你所在国家以外处理，包括新加坡、美国和中国。我们只提供每项服务完成工作所必需的数据。

你的选择和权利

  • 删除数据：在 App 里进入“我的 → 删除我的数据”。这会删除我们服务器上你的使用记录、待取回的报告和匿名账号，并清空手机上的拆解记录。为了不让你损失已付费的内容，我们会保留你剩余的已购次数和是否用过免费次数，这些只与随机设备 ID 关联。
  • 你可以发邮件并附上支持码，要求获取、更正或删除你的数据。
  • 你可以随时通过删除数据来撤回“把视频发送给 AI”的同意，App 在再次发送任何内容前会重新征求你的同意。

儿童

Shotwise 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集他们的数据。

安全

数据通过加密连接传输。上传的视频保存在只有我们服务器能访问的私有存储中，我们的系统访问权限受到严格限制。

政策变更

如果本政策有变更，我们会更新上面的日期。如果变更涉及你的视频如何处理，App 会在你继续使用前展示新的说明。
